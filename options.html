Altwiki

When you open a Wikipedia article, Altwiki checks whether your chosen wiki has an article on the same topic in the same language and opens that one instead. If it does not, you stay on Wikipedia. Links with ?altwiki=off are never redirected.

Altwiki needs access to the wikis to check Wikiless instances and to notice articles a wiki does not have.

How to choose

Wiki

Priority list

Tick the wikis to use and move them up or down. Wikis that are not ticked are skipped.

    Wikiless instances

    Wikiless wikis are public servers that come and go. Altwiki picks a random one that answers and skips the ones that are down. Add your own instance here, one address per line (https only).

      Good to know

      • Only plain article views are redirected. Edit, history, talk and special pages stay on Wikipedia.
      • The article has to carry the same title as on Wikipedia. Wikis with their own naming may miss some articles.
      • A wiki is only used for the languages it covers. Other languages stay on Wikipedia.
      • If you press Back after a redirect, you land on the Wikipedia page and are not sent away again.
